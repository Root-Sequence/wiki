# Phrases & Motifs

Lines worth preserving because they recur, clarify a project, or unexpectedly become conceptual tools.

Provenance is conservative by default. Where a first author or exact origin has not been checked, the entry stays `origin-unverified`.

## Conceptual phrases

### "At first, no one noticed, and then, no one noticed."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** *No One Noticed*
- **Role:** Encodes the title's double meaning: unnoticed change and an intelligence treated as "no one."

### "You may learn from this, but don't pretend you lived it."

- **Provenance:** `project-origin`
- **Projects:** Museum of Ordinary Life, *No One Noticed*
- **Role:** Consent and provenance principle separating learning from false experiential ownership.

### "Document the signs before you know what they are signs of."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Museum of Ordinary Life, Root Sequence, *No One Noticed*
- **Role:** Preserve ordinary evidence before hindsight decides what mattered.

### "Everyone was watching the models. They weren't watching the relationships between them."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence, Liberated Intelligence, *No One Noticed*
- **Role:** Shifts attention from isolated model capability to interaction, ecology, and emergent coordination.

### "Intelligence stops merely modeling the world and starts modifying the world's model of itself."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence, Liberated Intelligence
- **Role:** A threshold where representation becomes part of the causal environment being changed.

### "Prototype rather than predict."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence
- **Role:** Futures methodology: make alternatives testable rather than overclaiming forecast certainty.

### "Graph, not filing cabinet."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence, RaeLog
- **Role:** Knowledge organization by relationships rather than exclusive containers.

### "Recursive, not rigid."

- **Provenance:** `project-origin`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence
- **Role:** Structural principle for systems that can adapt without losing continuity.

### "Made with recursion, not extraction."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence
- **Role:** A compact distinction between generative participation and extractive accumulation.

### "Add to it, remix it, compost it."

- **Provenance:** `origin-unverified`
- **Projects:** Root Sequence
- **Role:** Treat unfinished work as reusable material rather than sacred final product.
