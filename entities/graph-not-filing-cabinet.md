# Graph, Not Filing Cabinet

Organize knowledge around relationships and traversable connections rather than forcing every idea into one exclusive container.

## Why it matters

Many Root Sequence ideas legitimately belong to several projects at once. A graph-oriented model lets each thing keep one identity while appearing through many relevant project, timeline, source, or thematic views.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** a general organization principle for interconnected work.
- **Root Sequence Wiki:** the architectural rule behind shared entities and generated project lenses.
