# Coherence Is a Systems Property

## Short definition

A public Root Sequence living paper, currently at `v0.1-dev`, examining what must be considered beyond an individual agent's objective to assess whether a change improves the conditions of a wider system.

**Working subtitle:** *Intelligence, Institutions, and the Conditions They Create*.

## Current status

The paper began on September 16, 2026 as an AI-assisted research scaffold. Its canonical source was rescued onto the reorganized repository structure and merged to `main` on September 18, 2026 at [`research/papers/coherent-systems/`](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/tree/main/research/papers/coherent-systems).

That merge establishes the durable source location; it does **not** establish a reviewed release. The manuscript remains `v0.1-dev`, author review is pending, it is not peer reviewed, no DOI exists, and no reader-facing website publication has been approved.

## Scope

The paper develops a bounded argument about how intelligent systems interact with institutions, infrastructure, material environments, affected people, assumptions, authority, consequences, and revision over time.

Its proposed contribution is a review method, not a universal coherence score or a claim to have solved sociotechnical alignment. Whether the method adds useful insight beyond competent existing systems practice remains an open research question.

## Relationship to the ecosystem

- **Root Sequence** owns the paper and tests, complicates, and revises it.
- Existing concept pages such as **Intelligence Ecology**, **Intelligence Institutional Ecology**, and **Legible Systems** remain canonical in their own homes; the paper synthesizes rather than replaces them.
- **Universal Coherence Framework** owns the detailed model/code it contributes to worked examples; the paper owns the synthesis and does not make UCF mandatory across Root Sequence.
- Private design or narrative projects may generate questions, but private material and fiction are not evidence for the public paper.

## Living-paper boundary

“Living” means substantive revisions remain reviewable, update the claims ledger and changelog, and retain contrary evidence. Git history records revision history; it does not by itself provide scholarly validation.

A reviewed release should identify its approved authors, version, date, and immutable revision. Until that gate is passed, references to the paper should preserve its draft status.
