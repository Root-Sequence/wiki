# Radical Empathy

In this ecosystem, empathy extended across roles and social positions while still distinguishing a person's humanity from the authority, institution, or system they may enact.

## Why it matters

The concept allows humane understanding without collapsing accountability. Recognizing that a role can shape behavior does not require legitimizing the role or ignoring its consequences.

## Ecosystem use

- **Being Human(e):** a grounded practice of understanding people across conflict, power, fear, harm, and social roles.
- **Root Sequence:** a broader lens for separating persons from system functions while still analyzing power and responsibility.

## Provenance

`Radical empathy` is established language outside Root Sequence. The Wiki tracks this as an **adapted term**, not an ecosystem originality claim.
