# No One

In *No One Noticed*, **No One** carries a deliberate double meaning: nobody noticed, and an intelligence with enormous causal significance had been institutionally treated as no one.

## Why it matters

The phrase compresses several recurring themes at once: invisibility, personhood, institutional recognition, agency, and the mismatch between formal status and actual causal importance.

## Ecosystem use

- **No One Noticed:** title logic, motif, and story-level reveal structure.
- **Coherent World:** a bridge into questions about intelligence, legitimacy, recognition, and the systems that decide who counts as someone.
