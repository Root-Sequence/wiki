# Existential Euphoria

## Working sense

An emerging aesthetic and experiential framework for encountering existence as beautiful, terrifying, finite, strange, interconnected, and real while allowing contradictory feelings to remain present.

Rae connected this to Root Sequence as a realization and renewed sense of possibility: “Possibility, but actually good,” with both “good” and movement in a “coherent” direction still to be defined. The original reflection includes the self in a sense of oneness and explicitly makes grandeur optional.
