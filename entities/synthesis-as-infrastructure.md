# Synthesis as Infrastructure

Synthesis treated as a maintained connective layer among knowledge, projects, decisions, and action rather than only as a one-off intellectual activity.

## Why it matters

A growing ecosystem can accumulate good ideas while still becoming unusable if relationships, contradictions, decisions, and destinations are not continuously made visible. In this sense, synthesis is part of the infrastructure that keeps the whole system navigable.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** a cross-project organizational and epistemic pattern.
- **Being Humane Atlas:** an applied example of turning fragments and observations into a traversable body of humane practice without flattening their context.
