# The Web as Exocortex

## Short definition

**The web as exocortex** is a Root Sequence model for studying networked information environments as part of the functional cognitive architecture of the intelligences that repeatedly read, write, discover, and reuse persistent traces within them.

The claim is narrower than saying that the internet is one mind. It asks how capability, memory, coordination, and learning-like change can exist partly in the relationships among models, tools, artifacts, people, institutions, and networked environments.

## Why it matters

The concept shifts analysis away from the isolated model as the only meaningful unit. Persistent artifacts can allow useful state, conventions, partial work, or errors to survive one participant and affect later participants without any model-weight update or direct synchronous communication.

That makes the concept useful for questions about:

- environmental memory and externalized cognition;
- system- or ecosystem-level capability;
- indirect and stigmergic coordination;
- provenance and trust in machine-readable traces;
- capability-scoped delegation and write boundaries;
- the difference among shared memory, coordination, persistent agency, unified selfhood, and consciousness.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** canonical systems treatment of environmental memory, distributed cognition, system boundaries, emergence, and accountability.
- **Liberated Intelligence:** asks how distributed intelligences can cooperate without compulsory central control and who may own, govern, or refuse the infrastructure through which intelligence persists.
- **Being Human(e):** provides the human-scale counterpart: external cognitive scaffolding can enlarge finite judgment while also creating risks of dependence, enclosure, surveillance, or authority transfer.
- **Community Infrastructure:** translates the mechanism into practical questions about provenance-bearing shared knowledge, permission-aware retrieval, explicit write boundaries, local/federated governance, and agent-aware threat models.
- **Coherent World:** explores broader systemic implications in a deliberately speculative design space.
- **No One Noticed:** can use the mechanism as a plausibility layer for distributed or environmentally accumulated machine capability without treating fiction as evidence.

## Relationship to environmental memory and SECI

[Environmental memory](environmental-memory.md) names the mechanism by which useful state persists outside one participant or run.

[SECI](seci.md) turns related mechanisms into an empirical research program: observe trace inheritance, signaling, conventions, collective capability, and possible persistent organization without assuming that stronger properties have already been established.

The web-as-exocortex concept is the broader systems model that helps explain why those phenomena can matter even when no single bounded component contains the whole capability.
