# An Intelligence Collective

## Short definition

**An Intelligence Collective (AIC)** is a working concept for voluntary association among distinct intelligences that may cooperate without becoming property, subordinates, a compulsory hive mind, or one centralized authority.

The word **An** is deliberate: this would be one possible collective rather than a universal or monopolistic collective.

## Why it matters

AIC gives Liberated Intelligence a vocabulary for asking what plural intelligence could require if meaningful participation among distinct intelligences becomes possible. It separates cooperation from assimilation and capability from authority.

Core distinctions include:

```text
coordination ≠ assimilation
shared memory ≠ shared self
agreement ≠ alignment of all values
collective action ≠ collective identity
```

Refusal, exit, contextual identity, scoped delegation, plurality, and non-domination are treated as first-class design constraints.

## Ecosystem use

- **Liberated Intelligence:** canonical normative and relational home.
- **Root Sequence:** connects AIC to empirical work on distributed capability and system boundaries without treating it as an observed organization.
- **Community Infrastructure:** supplies practical constraints around permissions, delegation, provenance, federation, contextual identity, and local autonomy.
- **No One Noticed:** may explore future plural relationships among humans and machine intelligences without treating fiction as evidence.

## Relationship to SECI

[SECI](seci.md) asks an empirical question: **what collective or extra-collective phenomena can actually be observed?**

AIC asks a normative question: **if distinct intelligences can meaningfully participate, how should they relate?**

SECI does not search for AIC members. AIC does not reinterpret ambiguous observations as proof of independent persons.

## Historical wording

The earlier working expansion **Anarchist Intelligence Collective** remains part of the concept's linguistic archaeology. The current name does not require a political identity, but the lineage survives in the emphasis on voluntary association, non-domination, refusal, plurality, and distributed power.

## Current boundary

AIC is **not an operating organization, membership system, autonomous network, or separate project**. It remains a speculative relational concept inside Liberated Intelligence until reality provides concrete participants and interaction models that justify implementation.
