# Coherent Communication

A working inquiry into developing, expressing, interpreting and changing meaning while preserving agency, differences and the ability to repair misunderstandings. Fidelity, generativity, intelligibility, truth and authorization remain distinct.

The [canonical manuscript](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/58fc7d694cc371bb57857d18f550ade40bb630bc/concepts/coherent-communication.md) owns the argument and proposed comparisons; the Wiki owns this route.
