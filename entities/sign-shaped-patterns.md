# Sign-Shaped Patterns

A pattern that has become suspiciously suggestive of something worth following without yet pretending it proves anything.

## Why it matters

The phrase is funny, but it also captures a useful epistemic middle state between random observation and overconfident conclusion: **this may be a sign; investigate before canonizing it.**

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** shorthand for a pattern worth tracing while preserving uncertainty.
- **Root Sequence Wiki:** an almost perfect label for heuristic signals that should prompt review rather than silently become facts.
