# Legible AI-Assisted Expression

## Short definition

A cross-project authoring principle: **AI should reduce the cost of expression without reducing the visibility of thought.** Assistance can help externalize, organize, connect, test, develop, and phrase an idea while keeping human-originated material distinguishable from AI-added inference, synthesis, transformation, and wording.

## Why it matters

Writing is not identical to thinking for everyone. AI may help serialize a large, nonlinear body of thought into language, or it may participate in thinking-through-writing. In either case, fluent prose can create premature coherence: an idea may look finished before its assumptions, contradictions, evidence, contribution history, or unresolved choices are visible.

The principle therefore treats provenance as part of accessibility and authorship rather than only as a disclosure at publication time. A useful trace preserves origin, transformations, factual support, uncertainty, decision points, review status, and the privacy boundary around source material.

“Show the work” means an inspectable artifact-level record—source fragments or authorized summaries, meaningful changes, added interpretations, evidence, alternatives, and decisions—not publication of hidden model reasoning or every private scratch note.
