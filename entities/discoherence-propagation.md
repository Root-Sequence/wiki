# Discoherence Propagation

The spread or amplification of incoherence through connected systems.

## Why it matters

A local mismatch between assumptions, signals, incentives, structure, and reality can become a network problem when other systems depend on, repeat, or optimize around it. The phrase gives that propagation process a handle without assuming a single mechanism.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** a systems-level pattern for tracing how failures or contradictions move across dependencies.
- **Universal Coherence Framework:** a possible application area if the concept is formalized and tested there rather than merely borrowing UCF language.
