# Bounded Structure

Enough structure for reliability and coordination without converting structure into coercion.

## Why it matters

The term is useful when a system needs dependable interfaces, roles, defaults, or boundaries but should not turn those mechanisms into permanent hierarchy or compulsory control.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** a general systems-design distinction.
- **Community Infrastructure:** a way to think about scoped roles, permissions, governance, and reliable coordination without universal authority.
- **Coherent World:** a design question for institutions that must remain dependable without hardening into domination.
