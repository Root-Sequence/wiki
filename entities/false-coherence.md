# False Coherence

## Short definition

Apparent order, agreement, stability, or successful coordination that looks coherent only because consequential disagreement, uncertainty, power, costs, or affected people have been flattened out of the visible system.

In the current public Root Sequence treatment, the clearest case is **manufactured consensus**: a group can produce a unanimous-looking result through dominance, fatigue, social pressure, informational cascades, or the cost of dissent even when the underlying judgments never genuinely converged.

## Coherence without conformity

False Coherence exists to preserve a distinction that recurs across the ecosystem:

> **Coherence is not conformity.**

A system can contain disagreement and still function coherently when the disagreement remains accurately represented, communication remains possible, coercive pressure is constrained, and the process can act without pretending that everyone shares one belief.

Conversely, visible agreement is not sufficient evidence of coherence. A vote total, consensus statement, smooth workflow, or stable outcome can conceal the mechanism that produced it.

## Ecosystem use

- **Root Sequence:** owns the broad public systems treatment of collective judgment, manufactured consensus, dissent, and decision-process integrity.
- **Universal Coherence Framework:** uses False Coherence as an optional analytical distinction among independent agreement, adaptive convergence, functional plurality, coordination without shared belief, and manufactured consensus.
- **Being Humane Atlas:** can examine the lived effects of dissent pressure, uncertainty, conformity, dignity, and protected judgment without becoming the public definition.
- **Community Infrastructure:** can test decision protocols, interfaces, permissions, decision memory, facilitation, and anti-domination safeguards.
- **Coherent World / No One Noticed:** may transform the concept into institutional or narrative questions without turning fiction into evidence.
- **Liberated Intelligence:** can ask how intelligent systems support deliberation without becoming the authority that decides which conclusions count as legitimate.

Project-specific treatments should transform or test the concept rather than create competing definitions.

## Boundaries

False Coherence is not a synonym for disagreement, compromise, majority rule, consensus, hierarchy, or any outcome the observer dislikes. The mechanism matters.

The concept should not be used as a moral score that automatically labels one faction coherent and another incoherent. It is useful only when the apparent order or agreement can be examined against the relationships, information flow, power, uncertainty, exclusions, and consequences that produced it.

## Current review frontier

A current Root Sequence draft review is testing whether the concept should extend more explicitly from manufactured consensus into coercive systems that appear orderly because affected people, suppressed feedback, externalized costs, or controlling relationships were excluded from the assessment boundary.

That broader synthesis remains under review in [`Root-Sequence/root-sequence#14`](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/pull/14). This Wiki entry therefore records it as a review frontier rather than silently treating the unmerged draft as settled canon.
