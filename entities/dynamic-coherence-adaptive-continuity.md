# Dynamic Coherence and Adaptive Continuity

## Short definition

A developing Root Sequence framework for asking how coherence should be assessed across change, feedback, scale, history, and time, and how a changing system can preserve or rebuild enough capability, relationship, organization, or lineage for meaningful continuation and future change to remain possible.

The framework separates **continuity** from simple sameness. A system can change substantially while remaining continuous in some relevant sense, and a system can preserve itself while narrowing the possibilities of people or systems around it.

## Current role

The September 24 public work narrowed this concept toward a **routing and synthesis role**, not a replacement discipline or universal mechanism-level analysis method.

Root Sequence's editorial T-003 pilots found little unique decision-relevant value from a Dynamic Coherence review when competent ordinary technical-incident methods already covered the mechanism. The resulting guardrail is to use established domain methods first and use the framework when it helps connect consequential questions those methods leave separated.

That makes the framework useful for questions such as:

- how earlier states or decisions alter later reachable possibilities;
- whether adaptation preserves or destroys future capability;
- what must remain stable and what must remain revisable;
- who can perceive, access, influence, refuse, or reshape reachable options;
- whether local adaptation pushes costs or constraints outside the chosen boundary;
- how feedback changes not only state but the way later state transitions occur.

## Boundaries

Dynamic Coherence and Adaptive Continuity is not a validated universal theory, a coherence score, a claim that organisms, institutions, minds, ecosystems, and artificial systems share one mechanism, or evidence that adaptation is automatically good.

Normative uses must name affected parties, boundaries, power, costs, and whose future possibilities are being preserved or narrowed.

The source framework also keeps consciousness, personal identity, legal identity, autobiographical continuity, organismic continuity, and functional persistence distinct rather than treating one as evidence for another.

## Provisional vocabulary

Two useful terms in the source remain deliberately **below entity maturity**:

- **agency envelope** — a provisional composite map across affordances, reachability, substantive opportunity, knowledge/perception, authorization, support/resources, causal influence, and power over conditions;
- **meta-agency** — a provisional umbrella for actions that alter later agency conditions through self-modification, environment modification, institutional change, or capacity building.

Both remain in the Wiki's public Seeds nursery rather than being promoted to standalone entities.

## Relationships

- **Root Sequence Design Grammar** uses adaptive continuity as a recurring design move.
- **Agency and Automation** asks which burdens can be removed while meaningful participation, refusal, inspection, recovery, and affected-party authority remain possible.
- **Legible Systems** and related autonomy work provide concrete design questions about future capability, repair, alternatives, and reversibility.
- **False Coherence** guards against calling persistence or target achievement coherent when power, coercion, suppressed feedback, or externalized costs fall outside the assessment boundary.
- **Conscious Systems** keeps continuity and subjective experience as related but non-identical questions.
- **Abandoned Futures** supplies a historical route into how earlier paths open, close, or leave recoverable possibilities.

Project-local implementations and mechanisms remain canonical in their own projects rather than being copied into this entity.

## Provenance

The intellectual-lineage review explicitly records that **dynamic coherence is not original to Root Sequence** and that **adaptive continuity** also appears in prior external work. The combined Root Sequence synthesis is therefore recorded conservatively as origin-unverified, not as a coinage claim.

The current title is also reviewable: the source considers keeping Dynamic Coherence, preferring Adaptive Continuity, using “dynamic-coherence grammar” descriptively, or renaming later. The Wiki preserves the current canonical document title and searchable component aliases without pretending the naming question is settled.
