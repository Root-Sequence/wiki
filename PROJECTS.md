## Shortest mental model

> **Root Sequence discovers. Community Infrastructure tests. Coherent World designs. No One Noticed experiences. The Museum of Ordinary Life preserves.**

Being Human(e), Liberated Intelligence, UCF, Liberation Mass, Good Chaos, the Wiki, and other projects cross that path rather than fitting into one linear sequence.

## Routing rule

The wiki explains **what a thing is and how it connects**. The relevant project repository owns the durable argument, implementation, canon, policy, archive record, or design.

When a wiki entry starts becoming the substantive work itself, move or link that work to its canonical project rather than letting the wiki silently become a second repository.
