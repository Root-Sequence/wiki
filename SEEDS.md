# Seeds

A nursery for terms, fragments, and connections that feel useful but do not yet deserve a full canonical home.

Seeds are allowed to be incomplete. They should still carry enough context that future-you can tell why they mattered.

## Current seeds

### Agency envelope

### Commons of Inquiry & Creation

- **Type:** proposed project / incubation seed
- **Provenance:** project-origin
- **Question:** What would it take to design, test, and eventually build a residential commons for inquiry, creation, experimentation, and ordinary life without prematurely turning an exploratory seed into an institution or fixed governance model?
- **Possible homes:** Temporary public bootstrap at Root-Sequence/root-sequence/incubator/commons-of-inquiry/; intended standalone Root-Sequence/commons-of-inquiry repository when established.
- **Related:** Root Sequence; Community Infrastructure; public project index.
- **Status boundary:** Public seed / incubation draft, not an operating institution, validated model, or permanent nested Root Sequence project.

## Seed format

```markdown
### Name

- **Type:** concept | phrase | motif | question | connection
- **Provenance:** origin-unverified
- **Question:** What is interesting or unresolved here?
- **Possible homes:** ...
- **Related:** ...
```

When a seed matures, move the substantive treatment to its canonical project and leave a pointer here or in the Lexicon.
